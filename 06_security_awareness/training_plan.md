# Cybersecurity Awareness Training Plan

## Audience and cadence

- All personnel: onboarding and annual refresher.
- Privileged users: semiannual role-based module.
- Executives and incident leaders: annual decision-making tabletop.
- Higher-risk teams: targeted reinforcement after trends or incidents.

## Core topics

1. Phishing, social engineering, and reporting.
2. Password managers, MFA, and credential protection.
3. Safe handling of confidential and regulated data.
4. Remote work, mobile devices, and physical security.
5. Incident reporting and the importance of rapid escalation.

## Measures

Track completion, quiz performance, phishing-simulation reporting, repeat failures, and post-training confidence. Use trends to select reinforcement topics.
