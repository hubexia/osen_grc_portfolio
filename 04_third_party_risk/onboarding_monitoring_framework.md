# Vendor Onboarding and Monitoring Framework

## Before contract signature

1. Determine criticality, data access, connectivity, and regulatory scope.
2. Issue the appropriate questionnaire and request supporting evidence.
3. Score control maturity, unresolved findings, and residual risk.
4. Add security, notification, audit, deletion, and subcontractor clauses to the contract.
5. Obtain risk acceptance for unresolved issues above tolerance.

## Ongoing monitoring

- High-risk vendors: quarterly issue review and annual reassessment.
- Medium-risk vendors: semiannual issue review and reassessment every two years.
- Low-risk vendors: annual confirmation and reassessment on material change.

Trigger an out-of-cycle review after a breach, material service change, ownership change, new data use, or significant assurance finding.
