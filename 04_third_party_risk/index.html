🤝 Third-Party Risk Assessment

This project demonstrates a repeatable third-party risk workflow from initial criticality screening through onboarding decisions and continuous monitoring.

Project Overview

ObjectiveEvaluate third-party vendors and suppliers for cybersecurity risk, control maturity, and onboarding readiness.
FrameworksISO/IEC 27001, NIST CSF 2.0, SOC reporting concepts
SkillsVendor risk management, security questionnaires, evidence review, vendor scoring, supply-chain security, monitoring
Evidence3 public-safe portfolio artifacts

Project Workflow

  1. Identified services, data access, integrations, and business criticality.
  2. Developed a risk-based security questionnaire.
  3. Reviewed SOC reports, certifications, policies, and supporting evidence.
  4. Classified vendors as low, medium, or high risk.
  5. Recommended contractual controls, remediation, and monitoring cadence.
