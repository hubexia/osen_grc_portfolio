# Vendor Security Questionnaire

## Organization and service

1. Describe the service, hosting model, support locations, and subcontractors.
2. Identify the customer data processed, stored, or transmitted.
3. Provide current security certifications or independent assurance reports.

## Governance and access

4. Is security responsibility assigned to a named executive or function?
5. How are workforce background checks and security training managed?
6. Describe identity lifecycle, MFA, privileged access, and periodic access reviews.

## Data protection

7. Describe encryption in transit and at rest, including key management.
8. Explain retention, deletion, backup, and secure-disposal practices.
9. Identify data residency and cross-border processing locations.

## Operations and resilience

10. Describe vulnerability scanning, remediation SLAs, and penetration testing.
11. Provide incident notification commitments and response contacts.
12. Summarize continuity, disaster recovery, and recent test results.
