# Internal Cybersecurity Assessment Report

## Executive summary

The sample assessment reviewed identity, logging, backup, vulnerability, and governance controls across critical business services. The most important improvement opportunities were centralized log monitoring and recovery-objective validation.

## Scope

- HR data platform
- Payment application
- Corporate email
- Supporting identity, network, and backup services

## Findings

### High — Centralized log monitoring is incomplete

Security-relevant logs are retained in separate platforms without consistent alerting or daily review. Implement a centralized monitoring workflow, assign review ownership, and define escalation thresholds.

### High — Backup recovery objectives are not validated

Documented RPO targets are not consistently supported by test evidence. Align backup frequency to approved RPOs and perform quarterly restoration tests.

### Medium — Access reviews are inconsistently evidenced

Managers review some privileged access, but evidence is not retained consistently. Establish a quarterly certification workflow and evidence repository.

## Conclusion

The assessment found a workable control foundation with targeted gaps in monitoring, recovery assurance, and evidence consistency. Remediation should prioritize high-risk findings and include control-owner validation before closure.
