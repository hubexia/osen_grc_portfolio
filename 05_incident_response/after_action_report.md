# Tabletop After-Action Report

## Scenario

A phishing compromise led to unauthorized mailbox access and attempted lateral movement into a shared business application.

## What worked

- The team identified an incident commander quickly.
- Technical responders agreed on initial containment priorities.
- Business owners understood the need to preserve evidence before recovery.

## Improvement actions

| Action | Owner | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create one maintained incident contact list | GRC | 30 days |
| Document authority for emergency account suspension | IAM | 45 days |
| Build a regulator and customer notification decision tree | Legal and Privacy | 60 days |
| Schedule a technical restoration exercise | IT Operations | 90 days |
