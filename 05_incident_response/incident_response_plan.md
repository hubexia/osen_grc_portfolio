# Incident Response Plan

## Purpose

Establish a coordinated process to identify, contain, eradicate, recover from, and learn from cybersecurity incidents.

## Roles

- Incident Commander: coordinates decisions, priorities, and status reporting.
- Technical Lead: directs investigation, containment, eradication, and recovery.
- Communications Lead: manages internal, customer, regulatory, and media communications.
- Legal and Privacy: assesses notification, evidence, and contractual obligations.
- Business Owner: sets service priorities and accepts recovery tradeoffs.

## Lifecycle

1. **Preparation:** maintain contacts, tools, playbooks, backups, and exercises.
2. **Detection and analysis:** validate signals, scope impact, classify severity, and preserve evidence.
3. **Containment:** isolate affected identities, endpoints, networks, or services.
4. **Eradication:** remove malicious access, close vulnerabilities, and reset compromised credentials.
5. **Recovery:** restore service, monitor for recurrence, and obtain business approval.
6. **Lessons learned:** document root cause, response performance, and assigned improvements.
