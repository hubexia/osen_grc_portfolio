# Ransomware Response Playbook

1. Isolate affected systems without destroying volatile evidence.
2. Disable compromised accounts and high-risk remote access paths.
3. Identify the ransomware family, initial access, lateral movement, and affected assets.
4. Preserve forensic images, logs, ransom notes, and relevant network evidence.
5. Validate clean backups and establish a prioritized recovery sequence.
6. Rebuild or restore systems only after eradication and credential rotation.
7. Monitor restored services and complete legal, insurance, and notification reviews.
