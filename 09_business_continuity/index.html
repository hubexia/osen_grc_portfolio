🔄 Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

This project demonstrates how critical business services, technology dependencies, recovery targets, and exercise plans are connected in a usable resilience program.

Objective: Develop business continuity and disaster recovery capabilities aligned to critical operations and agreed recovery expectations.

Project Workflow

  1. Identified critical business functions, dependencies, and disruption impacts.
  2. Established recovery time and recovery point objectives.
  3. Evaluated backup, alternate-site, and cloud-recovery options.
  4. Documented activation, communication, recovery, and return-to-normal steps.
  5. Recommended tabletop exercises and recovery simulations.
