# BC/DR Exercise Plan

## Annual cycle

- Quarter 1: contact-tree and crisis-communications test.
- Quarter 2: tabletop exercise for a critical service outage.
- Quarter 3: backup restoration and recovery-time validation.
- Quarter 4: integrated business and technology recovery simulation.

## Evaluation criteria

- Were decision roles and escalation thresholds understood?
- Were dependencies and recovery priorities accurate?
- Did recovery meet the agreed RTO and RPO?
- Were manual workarounds usable and sustainable?
- Were stakeholder communications timely and consistent?

Every exercise produces an after-action report with an owner, due date, and verification method for each improvement.
