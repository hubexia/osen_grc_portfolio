# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plan

## Activation

The crisis lead activates the plan when a disruption is expected to exceed a service threshold, create significant safety or compliance impact, or require coordinated recovery across teams.

## Priorities

1. Protect people and establish reliable communications.
2. Assess impact, affected services, dependencies, and expected duration.
3. Restore priority-one services within approved RTO and RPO targets.
4. Operate manual or alternate procedures where recovery is delayed.
5. Communicate status to employees, customers, suppliers, and regulators as required.
6. Validate service integrity before returning to normal operations.

## Recovery strategies

- Resilient cloud hosting and documented infrastructure recovery.
- Offline or immutable backups with routine restoration testing.
- Alternate work locations and remote-work procedures.
- Cross-trained staff and documented manual workarounds.
- Supplier contingency and escalation contacts.
