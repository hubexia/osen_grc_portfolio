# Risk Treatment Plan

## Scoring approach

Likelihood and impact are scored from 1 to 5. The combined result is reviewed with business context, existing safeguards, legal obligations, and service criticality before assigning a rating.

## Treatment options

- **Mitigate:** introduce or strengthen controls to reduce likelihood or impact.
- **Transfer:** shift part of the financial or operational exposure through contracts or insurance.
- **Accept:** document the rationale, approving authority, review date, and monitoring conditions.
- **Avoid:** discontinue the activity or technology that creates the exposure.

## Governance

Risk owners approve treatment actions and target dates. The GRC function reviews overdue actions monthly, validates closure evidence, and escalates risks that remain above tolerance.
