# Framework Recommendation Report

## Summary

NIST CSF 2.0 provides an accessible structure for communicating outcomes and maturity. CIS Controls v8.1 offers prioritized implementation safeguards. ISO/IEC 27001 supports a certifiable management system with formal governance and continuous improvement.

## Recommended adoption pattern

1. Use NIST CSF 2.0 as the executive and program-level outcome model.
2. Use CIS Controls v8.1 to prioritize technical and operational safeguards.
3. Use ISO/IEC 27001 to formalize governance, risk treatment, internal audit, management review, and improvement.

## Implementation sequence

- Establish scope, ownership, asset visibility, and risk criteria.
- Map current controls once and reuse evidence across frameworks.
- Prioritize gaps based on risk and business objectives.
- Define measures, review cadence, and accountable owners.
- Expand assurance only after the operating model is stable.
