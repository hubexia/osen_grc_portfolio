# Policy Governance Library

## Baseline policies

- Information Security Policy
- Access Control Policy
- Password and Authentication Standard
- Acceptable Use Policy
- Incident Response Policy
- Vendor Risk Management Policy
- Mobile Device Policy
- Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy

## Compliance-specific supplements

- HIPAA security and privacy requirements for healthcare data
- PCI DSS requirements for payment-card environments
- GDPR privacy and data-subject-rights considerations

## Lifecycle

Every policy follows draft, stakeholder review, approval, publication, awareness, annual review, and controlled retirement stages. Changes are versioned and linked to the framework or business requirement that triggered the update.
