# Policy Gap Analysis

## Scope

The review compared a sample policy inventory with ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A, CIS Controls v8.1, NIST CSF 2.0, and selected HIPAA and PCI DSS obligations.

## Priority findings

| Priority | Finding | Risk | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | No formal mobile device policy | Inconsistent enrollment, configuration, and data-handling practices | Approve a mobile device policy and supporting standard within 60 days |
| High | Legacy information security policy content | Current MFA, cloud, and vendor expectations are not fully represented | Refresh the policy and complete stakeholder approval |
| Medium | Duplicate acceptable-use language | Conflicting versions may create unclear expectations | Consolidate into one authoritative policy |
| Medium | Ownership fields are inconsistent | Review accountability may be missed | Assign a named role and annual review date to every policy |

## Remediation sequence

1. Confirm executive policy owner and approval authority.
2. Draft missing policies using the approved template.
3. Update authentication, third-party, and cloud-control language.
4. Consolidate duplicates and archive superseded documents.
5. Publish the library and schedule annual review reminders.
