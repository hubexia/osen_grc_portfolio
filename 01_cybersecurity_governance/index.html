📚 Cybersecurity Governance

This project demonstrates a repeatable policy lifecycle for researching requirements, assessing the current state, closing documentation gaps, and delivering an organized governance library.

Objective: Develop, review, and align cybersecurity policies and standards with organizational requirements, compliance obligations, and industry best practices.

Project Workflow

  1. Researched baseline and industry-specific policy requirements.
  2. Created a policy inventory with ownership, purpose, and review dates.
  3. Identified missing, outdated, and redundant documentation.
  4. Drafted and updated policies for MFA, vendor onboarding, and compliance expectations.
  5. Incorporated peer and stakeholder feedback before final delivery.

Portfolio Artifacts

FileDescription
policy_inventory.csv Sample inventory for policy ownership, purpose, status, and review cadence.
policy_gap_analysis.md Gap analysis with prioritized remediation recommendations.
